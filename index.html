Questions

What is Deluxe?

An electrolyte drink mix with energy and vitamins, in single-serve stick packs. The first one is called Ignite. Empty a stick into 16 to 20 oz of water, then stir or shake until dissolved.

How is it different from other electrolyte mixes?

Most are made only to rehydrate you. Deluxe has the electrolytes too, then adds 125mg of caffeine with L-theanine and a set of vitamins, so one stick covers the water and the wake-up.

Is it for the morning after a night out?

That is a big part of why we made it: water and electrolytes, plus caffeine for the fog. It is a drink mix, not a medicine, and it does not treat or prevent a hangover.

How much caffeine is in a stick?

125mg, from green tea extract. That is about one cup of coffee. Each stick also has 250mg of L-theanine. Not recommended for children or individuals sensitive to caffeine, pregnant, or nursing. Healthy adults should limit total daily caffeine intake to 400mg.
